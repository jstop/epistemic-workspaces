The zero point of a fallibilist epistemic network is a declared, non-neutral set of hinge commitments: constitutive of the network rather than claims within it, not defeasible inside the network, and revisable only by forking the protocol under a constituted authority. It comprises the initial state of every claim plus the bounds of the space, not the rules of motion within it. The hinge set has four members. (1) Prior: Dung's grounded semantics is the least fixed point from the empty set, so no claim carries default warrant; in gradual semantics the base-score assignment plays this role and is declared as the network's prior rather than hidden as a parameter. (2) Ceiling: no claim reaches certainty. This is the Resilience axiom of Amgoud and Ben-Naim: an argument with initial weight strictly inside (0,1) never reaches 0 or 1. It is enforced by one of two mechanisms whose selection is itself a hinge. (a) DF-QuAD with base scores strictly inside (0,1) and an acyclic support relation: under DF-QuAD's combination rule c = v0 + (1 - v0)(vs - va), self-support or mutual support has sigma = 1 as its only fixed point for every base score, so DF-QuAD is not Resilient on support cycles, and the DAG constraint is a structural prohibition of coherentist bootstrapping. (b) A Resilient gradual semantics implemented as a continuous dynamical system, for example Potyka's quadratic energy model (KR 2018): strength stays in [0,1] at all times, any reached equilibrium satisfies Resilience, and convergence is proven for acyclic graphs and for graphs containing only support relations, so a pure support cycle converges strictly below 1 and coherentist bootstrapping is defused arithmetically rather than by prohibition. The Euler-based restricted semantics of Amgoud and Ben-Naim is the acyclic exemplar of the same axiom set. Two limitations are carried as stated: for mixed attack-and-support cycles convergence is empirical only, and a discrete simultaneous update can oscillate where the continuized model converges, so option (b) means the continuized model. Any architecture that achieves the property by other means has still selected and declared a rule, and that selection is its hinge; the thesis claims these hinges are constitutive of this network, not that their contents are unique across all possible networks. (3) Floor: the signed attestation "key K signed P at t" is indefeasible only relative to the key event log; non-repudiation binds a key, not a person, and the key-to-person binding is supplied by the identity layer, not the argumentation layer. (4) Entry state: a claim enters at full commitment, default entitlement (its base score, which formalizes Brandom's prima facie entitlement) and zero earned entitlement, with earned entitlement (unchallenged / challenged / defended / conceded) tracked on a deontic ledger separate from graded strength. Declaring these hinges does not resolve the Munchhausen trilemma's dogmatic horn. It makes the dogmatic stop explicit and accountable, and relocates the question of its justification to the constituted authority that governs the protocol. The argumentation layer's zero point is therefore not self-grounding: its regress terminates wherever the identity and governance layer's does, and this thesis delegates that terminus rather than supplying it. The network's epistemic humility is itself a hinge, not a theorem of the network.
