Accountability and domination are separable: an identity system can guarantee that every action traces to an answerable human (accountability) while granting no authority any power to erase, expel, or punish that person (no domination). This is achievable by splitting self-certifying identity, which has no owner, from a verification-derived quality-of-binding assurance (IDQA), which holds no lever over the person and only attests how well the identity is bound to a real human.
